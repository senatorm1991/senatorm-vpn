# senatorm vpn – Privacy Policy (draft)

_Last updated: 2026-10-31_

senatorm vpn is an open-source VPN / proxy client (a fork of [Hiddify](https://github.com/hiddify/hiddify-app)).

## What the app does
- It creates a local VPN tunnel on your device (Android `VpnService`) and sends your traffic through the proxy servers / configurations **you** choose or import.
- It can download configuration lists from sources you enable, and test their latency/connectivity from your device.

## Data we collect
- **We do not operate any servers that receive your traffic, browsing history, DNS queries, or IP address.**
- We do not collect personal information, account data, or analytics, and we show no ads.
- Configurations, subscriptions and settings are stored locally on your device only.

## Third parties
- Traffic goes to the proxy server you connect to. That server's operator can see what any proxy operator can see; choose servers you trust.
- DNS requests use the DNS / DoH provider you select in Settings (default: Cloudflare). That provider's privacy policy applies.
- Config sources and the connectivity-test URL you use receive normal network requests from your device.

## Your control
You can delete all local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.
